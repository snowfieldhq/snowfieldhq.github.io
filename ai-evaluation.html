How we keep assistants honest.

Three checks stop assistants from inventing policies or drifting out of date as your operation changes.

Step 01

Settle the rules

We clean up conflicting playbooks first so answers come only from approved documents with the exact section cited.

Step 02

Verify with a second model

A second model from a different company (Claude, Gemini, or OpenAI) reviews every change before it reaches your staff.

Step 03

Hand off to a person

Anything outside the documented rules is flagged and sent to a named person instead of getting an invented answer.
