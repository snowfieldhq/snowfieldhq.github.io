What we build

One build, set up in your own phone and website accounts. You keep the accounts, the number and the records.

After-hours and overflow intake

An AI answering line on your existing number (or the answering tool you already pay for), plus your website form, asks the questions you would ask: is it leaking or backed up right now, what is the address, do they own or rent, can they send a photo. Urgent calls go to the one on-call alert path you choose. Estimate requests for sewer lines, water heaters and repipes come to you written up. You approve the questions before anything goes live.

Follow-up on big quotes

After a camera inspection or a water heater quote, up to four polite follow-ups over 14 days, only when the homeowner has agreed in writing to hear from you. They stop the moment the homeowner replies or opts out. At kickoff we agree how a quote gets added, such as forwarding the quote email, since we do not touch your dispatch software.

A one-page sewer conversation guide

For the sewer replacement talk at the kitchen table: what the camera showed, the options, and the financing questions to ask. Written from your own words, and gone over with your crew in a 30-minute session.

How it works

  1. A 15-minute intake review. You tell us how calls come in today and where jobs slip away. If you already have intake and quote follow-up that works for you, you do not need this, and we will say so on the call.
  2. Kickoff. We confirm your phone, form and software costs inside your accounts before anything is built.
  3. The build. The go-live date is agreed in writing once access, content and any texting registration are ready.
  4. Walkthrough and go-live. A live 30-minute session with your crew. It goes live when you approve it in writing.

Will it pay for itself?

The setup fee is one flat, one-time amount. We share it on the call and work this out with your numbers, not ours.

Jobs to break even = first-year cost ÷ what you keep on one job

First-year cost is the one-time setup fee, plus monthly care if you choose it, plus your own phone and software usage. What you keep on one job is the price after materials, labor and job costs. If your shop loses fewer jobs a year than it takes to break even, it is not worth it, and we will tell you.

The plain terms

  • One-time setup fee. Optional monthly care, which starts only after you approve the go-live in writing.
  • One phone route, one website form, one owner alert path, and the calendar or booking link you already use. We do not write into your dispatch software.
  • No texts to a homeowner without their written consent.
  • Monthly care includes one 30-minute copy or question edit; larger changes are quoted first.
  • Your phone, software and usage costs stay in your name and are confirmed before the build.
  • We promise the build, not a number of calls, jobs or dollars.
