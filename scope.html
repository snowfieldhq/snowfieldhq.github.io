Client account ownership.

All model workspaces, repositories, prompts, evaluation sets, and documentation are created inside accounts you own and control. Snowfield operates through revocable access during implementation. When an engagement ends, your workflows and data stay intact in your environment.

Signal audit before build.

Each engagement begins with a written scope identifying the operational bottleneck, reference documents, model stack (Claude, Gemini, or OpenAI), tooling (Python, React, or existing enterprise systems), and measurable acceptance checks.

Independent verification.

Before handover, workflows are tested against real operational edge cases with independent cross-model review checks. Assistants cite approved sources and flag gaps when information is missing.

Live team onboarding.

Delivery includes plain-language documentation and live team onboarding so your staff can update playbooks, adjust rules, and run daily operations themselves.
