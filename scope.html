What we set up.

Snowfield connects one agreed phone route and one existing website inquiry form to intake questions, one owner alert and your existing booking link or a booking request for your team to confirm. The written scope names each connection. A booking request does not reserve an appointment or write to your calendar. We configure eligible follow-up, up to four messages over 14 days, only for the purpose and consent you have documented. Follow-up stops on reply, booking or opt-out. Without eligible consent, those texts stay off.

Price.

Setup is one payment of $1,150, paid on the call after you review these terms. Support is $297 per month, starting only when you accept go-live in writing. You pay software, registration, phone and usage bills directly. Illustrative scenario, prices checked November 12, 2026: one local number, the $97 monthly software plan, 1,000 text segments and 200 forwarded call minutes a month give software and usage of about $121.75 to $131.20 monthly and registration of about $23.48. This includes a 5% markup that one provider guide applies only with re-billing; another provider page lists registration at $22.50 with no markup. The illustrative first-year total, including setup and 12 support months, is $6,198.42 to $6,311.82. Actual usage, registration category, billing settings, taxes, existing carrier charges and optional features can change this total. We confirm your applicable software costs in your own account before payment; the example is not a spending cap.

Ownership.

You keep your accounts, number and records. Snowfield uses revocable access. Cancellation ends Snowfield support; your configuration and data remain in your account.

Support.

Email support includes a monthly check of routing, opt-outs and the booking handoff, a short results summary, and one copy or intake-question change taking up to 30 minutes. Support is handled by Snowfield's AI assistants following a fixed monthly checklist; we do not promise human review of each message. Additional routes, forms, integrations, campaigns and redesigns need a separate scope. Response time, message delivery speed, bookings and revenue are not guaranteed.

Dates and acceptance.

After access, approved sender registration and agreed content are available, we agree a go-live date in writing. Before activation we send evidence that the named phone and form routes, intake, alert and booking handoff work, and that eligible follow-up stops on reply, booking and opt-out. You can accept by replying "I accept go-live for [scope version] on [date]," or identify a failed included function. Silence is not acceptance. Included defects are repaired and retested without another setup fee. We do not activate ongoing support or its charge before written acceptance. If you have not replied 30 calendar days after we send evidence, we close setup and refund unearned milestones; your accounts stay intact.

Cancel before work.

Email the support address shown on your order; a phone call is not required. If the agreed scope cannot fit, or you cancel before work starts, we refund the full $1,150.

Cancel after work starts.

For a change of mind or unresolved customer/provider dependency, we refund unfinished milestones: $230 for the access and route plan, $690 for configuration and demonstration, and $230 for accepted go-live. These allocate the single setup payment for refunds; they are not installment payments. We retain those amounts only after supplying the route plan, supplying passing configuration test evidence, or recording written acceptance, respectively. Partial milestones are refunded in full. The allocations total $1,150.

If our delivery fails.

If Snowfield cannot make an included function pass acceptance, or you report a reproducible failure within 30 calendar days after accepted go-live, give us access and 10 business days to repair and retest it. If it still fails for a reason within our control, you may cancel for the full setup fee, less any setup refund already received, plus the current support month's fee and unused prepaid future support. This also applies if we miss the agreed go-live date for a reason within our control and do not deliver passing evidence within that repair window after your notice.

If a dependency prevents delivery.

Customer changes, missing access, unavailable software and carrier approval or delivery failures are dependencies; we document them rather than count them as successful delivery. The repair clock pauses only while a documented dependency actually prevents retesting. If a dependency remains unresolved for 30 calendar days, either party may end setup using the unfinished-milestone refund. You can cancel earlier using that same calculation. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding United States federal holidays.

Later support defects.

After the first 30 days, included-function defects receive repair within support. If unresolved after 10 business days with required access, you may end support for a refund of the current support month's fee plus unused prepaid future support; this does not create a new setup refund.

Refund timing and unused support.

Cancellation stops future support charges on a future date you request, or the date we receive your email otherwise. We refund unused support by the calendar days remaining in its billing period, starting on that date. If more than one refund covers the same money, we pay the largest, not the sum. Refunds never exceed the amount paid for that item. We initiate refunds within 10 business days after they become due; your provider controls posting time. We charge no cancellation fee or processing-fee deduction. Software bills remain subject to their providers' terms.

Monthly payment.

Support runs month to month with no minimum term. Its first period starts on accepted go-live; later periods start on its monthly anniversary, using the month's last day if needed. We email monthly invoices. Each invoice is due on the first day of its support period. If an invoice is unpaid 14 days after its due date, support pauses; your configuration stays in your account. Setup payment alone does not authorize automatic support debits. Any automatic payment requires separate, recorded authorization of the amount, start date and renewal schedule, and a tested cancellation method. The order identifies the support/cancellation email and billing method before payment. Software subscriptions continue unless separately canceled with their providers. These terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be limited.
