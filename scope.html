Four operating commitments.

Clear boundaries from discovery through team handoff, with no proprietary lock-in.

01 · Account control

Client account ownership

All AI workspaces, code repositories, prompts, test cases, and documentation are created inside accounts you own and control. Snowfield operates through revocable access during implementation. When an engagement ends, your workflows and data stay intact in your environment.

02 · Written scope

Audit before build

Each engagement begins with a written scope identifying the operational bottleneck, reference documents, AI models (Claude, Gemini, or OpenAI), supporting tools (Python, React, or your existing systems), and clear pass-or-fail checks.

03 · Pre-handoff checks

Independent verification

Before handoff, workflows are tested against real operational edge cases and reviewed by a second model from a different company. Assistants cite approved sources and flag gaps when information is missing.

04 · Team readiness

Live team onboarding

Delivery includes plain-language documentation and live team onboarding so your staff can update playbooks, adjust rules, and run daily operations themselves.
