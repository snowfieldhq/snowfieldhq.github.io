Acceptance, support and refunds

You keep your accounts, number and records and grant revocable access. A booking request needs your team to confirm it; it does not reserve a time or write to your calendar. Eligible follow-up stops on reply, booking or opt-out; texts without documented consent stay off. AI assistants will provide the monthly routing, opt-out and booking check, a short summary and one copy or intake-question change up to 30 minutes. Human review of every message, response time, delivery speed, bookings and revenue are not promised. Added routes, forms, integrations, campaigns and redesigns need separate scope.

We supply passing evidence for the included functions before activation and repair included defects without another setup fee. Only your explicit written go-live acceptance starts support and billing. Silence is not acceptance; after 30 calendar days without a reply to the evidence, setup closes and unearned milestones are refunded, with your accounts intact.

If the scope cannot fit, or you cancel before work, the full $1,150 is refunded. After work starts, a change of mind or unresolved dependency refunds unfinished milestones: $230 for access and route plan, $690 for configuration and demonstration, and $230 for accepted go-live. These allocate the one setup payment for refunds, not installments. Only supplied plan, passing configuration evidence and written acceptance earn the respective amounts; partial milestones are refunded in full. A dependency unresolved for 30 calendar days lets either party end setup on this basis; you can cancel earlier on the same basis.

If an included function cannot pass acceptance, or you report a reproducible failure within 30 calendar days after accepted go-live, give us access and 10 business days to repair and retest. If it still fails within our control, cancellation refunds the setup fee (less any setup refund already received), plus the current support month's fee and unused prepaid future support. This also applies to a missed agreed go-live date within our control without passing evidence in that repair window after notice. Documented dependencies pause the repair clock only while they prevent retesting. Later support defects unresolved after 10 business days with required access allow cancellation for the current support month and unused prepaid future support, without a new setup refund. Business days exclude weekends and United States federal holidays.

Cancel by email to the support address on your order; a call is not required. Cancellation stops future charges on email receipt or your requested future date. Unused support refund = amount paid x calendar days remaining / calendar days in the period. For the same money, the largest refund applies, not their sum; each item is capped at its paid amount. Refunds are initiated within 10 business days after due, without a cancellation fee or processing deduction; provider posting times vary. Your software bills remain subject to their providers' terms.

Support is month to month with no minimum. Its first period starts on accepted go-live; later periods start on the monthly anniversary, or month-end if needed. We email monthly invoices, each due on the first day of its support period. An invoice unpaid 14 days after its due date pauses support; your configuration stays in your account. Setup alone does not authorize automatic support debits. Automatic payment requires separate recorded authorization of amount, start date and renewal schedule plus a tested cancellation method. Software subscriptions continue unless separately canceled. These terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be limited. Review the full written scope and your actual account costs before payment; illustrative cost scenarios are not spending caps.
